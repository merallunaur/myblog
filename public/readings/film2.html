"The Substance" (2024) has a very famous heavy cult-film flavour. One of its features is the use of distorted camera lenses to present the male gaze and gender issues. It is very direct. I think there is no hierarchy between metaphor and directness—it is not that making the viewer guess is more sophisticated, it is just a different mode of presentation. I actually hate being a riddler. When I watched the shot-by-shot analysis, I also saw that the camera language uses many mirrors and angles to reflect the dynamic relationships between characters. The score is also a highlight, a layered humming sound like insects or machinery. The rhythm also gives a sense of urgency, reminding me of a YouTube edit about the Bosnian War, with music that skips like an alarm or a heart monitor. The directness of the theme fits the film's pure horror expression—blood, organs falling off, etc. It also accidentally created the famous meme "Nà zhǒng wùzhì" (That Substance). 《某種物質》（2024）很有名的濃厚cult片味，一個特點就是用畸變鏡頭展現男凝問題和性別問題。不過是非常直給的，我覺得隱喻和直給沒有高下之分，不是說讓觀者猜就更加高級，只是呈現方式不同。我其實很討厭當謎語人。看拉片的時候也看到鏡頭語言中運用很多鏡子與夾角來體現角色之間的動態關係。還有配樂也是一大亮點，一種類似昆蟲，機械的有層次的嗡鳴聲。節奏也給人一種緊迫感，讓我想到油管上一個波黑戰爭的剪輯視頻，也是這種跳得像警報或是心電監護儀的音樂。直給的主旨很符合這部電影純粹的恐怖表現，血，器官脫落等等。還陰差陽錯地創造了名梗「娜種物質」。
"Beau Is Afraid" (2023). Two or three years ago? A very long, boring, stream-of-consciousness presentation. It portrays the conflict and fear toward family to the fullest. I estimate most East Asian children can relate. There are many metaphors and hints in the imagery; you can tell the director had a great time filming. At the time it sparked controversy over the director's personal expression versus audience reception. For me, although the presentation is quite personal, I can still feel the emotions the director wanted to express. Some chapters are presented in a very children's-theatre or fairy-tale way. I feel this kind of presentation generally exists in the depiction of the grotesque or the uneasy, but in this film it should be a way of confronting trauma. Similar to how a young child who suffers sexual abuse might imagine their experience as Hansel and Gretel, to fight against an unbearable harm. If you don't understand it, watching it straight through can be a bit boring; you can watch a shot-by-shot analysis. 《博很恐懼》（2023）兩三年前？的電影，很長時間的無聊的意識流表現。把對家庭的矛盾和恐懼展現得淋漓盡致，我估計是大多數東亞小孩能共情的一部。畫面中的隱喻和暗示都很多，能看出來導演拍爽了。當時就引發了對導演個人表達和觀眾接受取捨方面的爭議。對於我而言雖然呈現方式比較個人化，我還是能感受到導演想表達的情緒的。有些篇章的呈現非常兒童劇化，或者童話化。感覺這種表現一般都存在於怪誕或者不安的呈現，不過這個片子里應該是對抗創傷的表現。類似於一個幼童遭受性侵時，他可能會把自己的經歷想象成漢塞爾和格雷特，以此對抗這種無法接受的傷害。如果不懂自己看下去會有點無聊，可以看拉片。
"Death of a Unicorn" (2025). A terrible film. A very low-value slasher-type film, except the killer is a unicorn. "Sinners" (2025). I thought Jordan Peele directed it. First of all, MBJ is so handsome. The music montage section is the essence of the film's theme of racial inclusivity; the fusion of music + colonial conflict + vampires + the West is very novel. It is a film that can provoke thought; the different fates of the two protagonist brothers might also illustrate that marginalized groups must give up something to integrate into mainstream society. And neither path is a good ending. It is a film worth watching! Even just for MBJ's face. 《罪人》（2025）我還以為喬丹皮爾拍的。首先麵包機好帥。音樂蒙太奇那一段是展現電影種族包容主旨的精華，音樂+殖民矛盾+吸血鬼+西部的融合很新穎。是一部能引發人思考的電影，主角兄弟兩的不同命運可能也就說明了邊緣群體融入主流社會必須要放棄某些東西。並且哪一條都不是好結局。是一部值得看的電影！衝著麵包機老公的臉也可以看。
"Bring Her Back" (2025). A kitchen knife growing into the gums looks so painful!! The film basically lays out the female protagonist's scheme openly, but it still makes you feel terror and suffocation. In a family environment that should be full of love and support, the male protagonist falls into an isolated and helpless situation. Watching it makes your blood pressure spike. The brother and sister are so pitiful. But aside from the body horror segments, the film's core doesn't hold up to scrutiny. I really hate this kind of film where one person's selfish desire drags in many people; maybe that's one of the themes. But I don't think she deserves any sympathy at all, and she didn't get the punishment she deserved. 《復活她》（2025）菜刀生卡牙齦看著很痛啊！！影片對女主角的陰謀基本上是明牌呈現，但依舊讓人感覺到恐怖和窒息。在家庭這種本該充滿愛與支持的環境裡，男主角陷入孤立無援的境地。看得人血壓蹭蹭往上漲。哥妹好慘。但是除了身體恐怖的橋段，影片的內核經不起推敲。我很討厭這種因一己私慾捲入很多人的電影，可能這是主題之一吧。但我覺得一點都不值得同情，她也根本沒有得到應有的懲罰。
"Together" (2025). How was it promoted as comparable to The Substance? Very average. "Identity" (2003). At a time when split personality is no longer a niche and novel subject, this film still uses its exquisite plot and suspense setup to make the audience completely fall into it. The ending is unexpected yet reasonable, reminding me a bit of the twist in Dead Silence; the feeling is similar. I don't know whose perspective is correct in this film, because the protagonist's perspective is completely subjective—we do not watch this film as bystanders. So I kept guessing whether the protagonist killed someone without realizing it. 《致命ID》（2003）在人格分裂不是小眾新穎題材的當下，這部電影依舊用他精妙的劇情與懸念設定使觀眾完完全全陷進去了。結局出乎意料又合乎情理，讓我有點想到看死寂時的反轉，感覺上差不多。這部電影不知道誰的視角是正確的，因為主角的視角是完全主觀的，我們並不以旁觀者的身份觀看這部電影。所以我當時一直在猜是不是主角無意之間殺人。
"Eddington" (2025). I don't like it. I wouldn't call it disappointing either; anyway, you can feel that Ari Aster definitely had a great time filming, and that's good. It feels like a post-pandemic reflection on the pandemic era, but maybe my comprehension is insufficient; I only saw a conspiracy theory packaged as a DLC. 《愛丁頓》（2025）不喜歡。也談不上失望，反正能感覺阿里艾斯特本人肯定拍爽了，這就很好了。華金演窩囊廢一直可以的。我不太明白想要表達什麼，感覺就是一個後疫情時代對疫情時代的反思，但是可能也是我理解功力不足，只看到了一個陰謀論打包dlc。
"Babygirl" (2024). I've never seen such a filthy film filled with the director's vulgar sexual fantasies about powerful, successful women. Revolting. But who hasn't wanted to take an axe and chop down everyone ahead of them in line when they can't find a job? 《選擇有罪》（2025）很喜歡的一部翻拍電影。其實對韓國本土的職場思考融入的不是非常多。比起現實諷刺片，可能在我這裡的定位更偏向於灰色幽默喜劇片。不過找不到工作的時候誰又不想拿著斧頭把排自己前面的人都砍了呢。
"Regretting You" (2025). A fairly typical American family comedy. I'm gradually discovering I'm not the audience for this kind of film anymore. 《愛•憾事》（2025）比較典型的美式合家歡。當時看墮落天使重映的時候看到了預告，因為有眼熟的演員麥肯娜+逃出絕命鎮的女主角，而且看起來是比較直白的主題。也確實是拍過無數次的愛人錯過，錯過的愛人被自己的伴侶雙雙背叛，最後愛人不錯過的劇情。很無聊，漸漸發現這種片子我不是受眾了。

"Raise the Red Lantern" (1991). A film from a long time ago; I think it's Zhang Yimou's peak. This year I watched it because I'm writing a Republican-era novel. The film's symmetry and cold-warm composition are very aesthetically pleasing, and the symbolisation of the master is also very inspiring. The film moves the original novel from Jiangnan to northern Shanxi, which is a very successful adaptation. The Jiangnan courtyard, because of its landscaping, is actually more lively, while that Shanxi courtyard is tall and dark, like a carved coffin stone. Also, the music every time someone dies should come from folk songs, with a relatively bright rhythm, but it sounds especially scary. Some note must have been deliberately raised by a semitone, very dissonant. 《大紅燈籠高高掛》（1991）很久以前的電影，我覺得是老謀子的巔峰了。今年因為要寫一個民國長篇才來看。影片的對稱和冷暖構圖非常具有美感，同時對於老爺進行符號化也非常具有啟發性。電影把原著從江南搬到北方山西省，是一次非常成功的改編。江南的院子因為造景其實還是比較靈動的，而山西的那個院子又高又暗，就像一塊雕好的棺材石頭。還有每次死人的那個配樂，應該是來自民歌，比較明快的節奏，但是聽起來特別嚇人。應該是哪個音刻意地升半音了，非常不和諧。
