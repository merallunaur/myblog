The Olfactory File 嗅覺檔案

Currently I use Moonmilk by Stora Skuggan and Moon Bloom by Hiram Green on a daily basis. Because I love the moon so much, the names alone were enough to attract me. 現在一直在用的是Stora Skuggan的Moonmilk和Hiram Green的Moon Bloom，因為我很喜歡月亮，所以在名稱上就非常吸引我。
For daily wear I use Stora Skuggan Moonmilk (2015). It is a relatively fresh and crisp white floral, with a faint, elusive softness. It is like full, satin-like richness, yet carries a strong sense of distance. Because I do not want everyone to get close to me, but I also want the scent to not stand out too much, I chose this one for everyday. Its top notes are black tea and lime; heart notes are cardamom, lily of the valley, mandarin and black pepper; and base notes are Mysore sandalwood and light leather. The brand is a Stockholm-based niche perfume studio founded in 2015, and the scent is inspired by the 1555 description of "moonmilk" by Swiss naturalist Conrad Gessner—the creamy, pearlescent substance that seeps from limestone caves under moonlight. A sunny scent with a nighttime shadow. 其中日常會用Stora Skuggan的Moonmilk（2015），是比較清新幹練的白花香，同時也有一絲若有似無的柔和。比較像絲緞一樣飽滿的，但有一種強烈的距離感。因為我不想讓所有人都靠近我，但是也想要香味沒那麼出眾，所以會選這支日常。前調是紅茶與青檸；中調是小豆蔻、鈴蘭、柑橘與黑胡椒；後調是邁索爾檀香與輕皮革。品牌是2015年創立於斯德哥爾摩的小眾香水工作室，靈感來自1555年瑞士博物學家康拉德·格斯納對「月乳」的描述——月光下從石灰岩洞穴中滲出的乳白色珍珠狀物質。一款帶著夜色陰影的陽光香氣。
Moon Bloom by Hiram Green (2013) is a rich, intense floral, with a kind of primal animality. Smelling it gives a dizzying happiness. It is my ideal date-night scenario! Even though I have not been in a relationship for six years. The scent is built around tuberose, the "mistress of the night," blended with jasmine absolute, ylang-ylang, coconut, leafy greens, tropical spices and resins. It is the debut fragrance of Hiram Green, a Canadian natural perfumer based in the Netherlands, who uses only natural ingredients. Hiram Green的Moon Bloom（2013）就是濃郁的花香，有種原始的動物性，聞起來有種讓人幸福的頭暈感。是我理想的約會場景！雖然我6年沒談戀愛。這支香以晚香玉——「夜晚的女主人」——為核心，混合茉莉淨油、依蘭依蘭、椰子、綠葉、熱帶香料與樹脂。它是Hiram Green的出道作品，這位加拿大天然調香師定居荷蘭，只用天然原料。
Loewe 001 Woman (2016). Whether it is "after the morning after" I have not experienced, but smelling it for a long time does make one a little addicted. It is a fruity floral, but I expected it to be a lazier scent—it is not. For me it is neither here nor there. 羅意威事後清晨女香（2016）。事不事後沒經歷過，但聞久了確實有點上頭。是水果花香，但以為會是聞起來比較慵懶的香調，但不是。在我這裡無功無過。
Dior Poison (1985). I expected something more uniquely gourmand, but it turned out to be a salty cream scent. A large part of my judgment of gourmands comes from whether I find them tasty. This one—not tasty. 迪奧紅毒（1985）。本以為是更加獨特的美食調，結果是鹹奶油的味道。我對美食調的判斷很大一部分來源於我覺得好不好吃。這個，不好吃。
Dior Sauvage (2015). When I am in a bad mood I spray this. In my impression it is a men's fragrance. It feels like the scent of a stereotypical domineering CEO. Every cliché of men's fragrance is present: leather, tobacco, roughness, and so on. Three wishes granted in one. 迪奧曠野（2015）。我心情不好就會噴這支。印象裡是男香，感覺就是刻板印象霸總身上的味道。所有男香的刻板印象它都有：皮革、煙草的粗糙等等。三個願望一次滿足。
YSL Mon Paris EDP (2016). Too sweet—so sweet I truly cannot resist it. The fruity note is too prominent. If I had a boyfriend, I feel this would be a scent for people who love sweet girls. I also really like it. Occasionally I play sweet girl. 楊樹林反轉巴黎EDP（2016）。太甜了，甜得讓我實在無法抗拒。水果香氣過於突出。如果有男朋友，感覺是i甜妹人士喜歡的。我也很喜歡，偶爾裝甜妹。
YSL Black Opium EDT (2014). The sample did not say, but it feels like it is not the intense version. It is like a flower blooming on just-burned charcoal—a cold, icy scent. I will try the intense version later. I prefer intense versions. 楊樹林黑鴉EDT（2014）。小樣沒寫，但感覺不是濃香那款。像是剛剛焚燒過的碳上開出的花，冷冰冰的香氣。會以後試試濃香款，我更愛濃香。
YSL Black Opium Blue (2019?). I forgot which kind it was—after all, I lost it in Beijing. It is the smell of me drinking too much at a bar. Meaning I did not smell of alcohol. I vaguely remember a coffee note, or maybe a liqueur note. 楊樹林藍鴉（2019？）。忘了是哪種了，畢竟丟在北京了。我在酒吧喝多了的味道——意思是我沒有酒臭味。依稀記得有股咖啡味還是利口酒的味道。
L'Artisan Parfumeur Passage d'Enfer (1999). It really did send me off, but not in the way I imagined—pulling out my organs, drowning me in spices, wrapping my corpse in cloth to sleep by the Nile. It was the way of several dozen old nuns chanting sutras beside me to deliver my soul. Not a peaceful departure. 阿蒂仙冥府之路（1999）。確實把我送走了，但不是以我想的那種送走——掏出我的內臟，把我溺在香料裡，然後用布裹起來，讓我的屍體沉睡在尼羅河畔。是幾十個老尼姑在我旁邊念經給我超度的那種送走。走得不怎麼安詳。
Diptyque Tam Dao (2003). Same as above, but better. It feels like my grandmother stroking my head and chanting sutras. 蒂普提克檀道（2003）。同上，但是好點。感覺是我奶奶摸著我的腦袋念經。
Diptyque Do Son (2005). The narcissus note is too powdery and thick. I feel like I am about to travel back to my true form as Miss Blue Iris singing Qinhuai River in a Shanghai cabaret. 蒂普提克杜桑（2005）。水仙的味道脂粉氣太重太厚了。感覺馬上要穿越回我的真身——大上海歌舞廳藍鳶尾小姐演唱秦淮河。
Diptyque Rose (2005?). A very rare rose scent that is not too pungent. It feels like a dew-covered rose crushed on the branch. A little pure, innocent, and decadent. 蒂普提克玫瑰（2005？）。非常難得的一款玫瑰氣味不太沖人的香水。感覺是在花枝上就被碾碎了的、沾著露水的玫瑰花。有點純淨天真的糜爛。
Diptyque Olène (1988). It smells good, but it is a bit too pungent. It feels like something used in India to blend certain couple's products. 蒂普提克清晨百合（1988）。好聞是好聞，有點太沖了。感覺像是印度那邊調某種情侶用品用的。
Diptyque Philosykos (1996). It is just the smell of fig plus leaves. No surprise. I do not like it. 蒂普提克無花果（1996）。就是無花果加葉子的味道。沒什麼驚喜。不喜歡。
Diptyque Do Son (again, listed separately by the author). The lily note is quite obvious; the green leaf note is also fresh. Not too much industrial trace. 蒂普提克清晨百合（再次）。百合花香挺明顯的，綠葉香也很清新。沒有太重的工業痕跡。
Serge Lutens L'Orpheline (2014). On first spray there is a smoky note—really 5% of secondhand smoke. But later it becomes both sweet and cool. Not someone to mess with either. Essential for meeting strangers. 蘆丹氏孤兒怨（2014）。初噴上一股煙味，是真的二手煙的5%的煙味。但是後面又甜又涼，也不是好惹的貨色。見陌生人必備。
Serge Lutens La Fille de Berlin (2013). The most disappointing one. Very popular, very hyped as "the girlish body scent seeping from the bones of a mature woman." But it feels more like rose-scented Florida water? The girl directly bashes her head against the wall. Crazy. Scammed. 蘆丹氏柏林少女（2013）。最失望的一個。很火很火，號稱熟女骨子裡透出來的少女體香，但感覺更像玫瑰味花露水？少女直接拿頭撞牆。神經病吧，詐騙了。
Serge Lutens Chêne (2004?). It has the smell of furniture waxed with beeswax. 蘆丹氏北非東風（2004？）。有種家具打蜜蠟的味道。
Serge Lutens La Religieuse (2015). Similar to L'Orpheline. Feels like a nun's corpse has gone bad. 蘆丹氏修女（2015）。跟孤兒怨差不多。感覺修女的屍體臭了。
Serge Lutens Féminité du Bois (1992). Why does your house only have the smell of incense? 蘆丹氏林之嫵媚（1992）。為什麼你家只有焚香一種味道？
Ariana Grande Moonlight (2017). The bottle's iridescent finish is beautiful, like a purple grenade. The scent is very much like apricot cake. It feels like traveling into The Nutcracker. 愛莉安娜月光（2017）。瓶子偏光很好看，像個紫色手雷。味道很像杏子蛋糕，有種穿越進胡桃夾子的感覺。
Juicy Couture Viva la Juicy (2007). Similar to the above, but with a stronger caramel note. 橘滋甜美萬歲（2007）。跟上面味道差不多，但是焦糖味濃一點。
Juicy Couture Dirty English (2008). Orange Fanta. 橘滋髒話（2008）。橙子味芬達。
Aquolina Pink Sugar (2003). When sprayed and smelled up close, there is a cheap plastic chemical note. The sweetness is fine for me, but should be too high for most people. It really is the smell of fruit candy. 水蓮娜粉紅棉花糖（2003）。一噴出來湊近聞會有一股劣質塑料化學品味道。甜度對我還好，對大多數人應該偏高了。確實是水果糖的味道。
Prada Candy Night (2012). The chocolate note in the base is particularly charming, but the bitter orange top note really has a chemically synthesized sulphur-fumigated Chinese medicine smell. 普拉達糖果之夜（2012）。後調的巧克力味特別迷人，但前調的苦橙實在有股化學合成熏硫中藥味。
Kenzo Flower (2000). Iris and powder notes are very strong. Relatively coquettish. 凱卓一枝花（2000）。鳶尾和脂粉味很濃。比較風塵。
Jean Desprez Bal à Versailles (1962). Feels like many spices mixed together. I am like a doorman squeezed under the armpits of noblewomen attending a court ball before the French Revolution. All kinds of scents rush at my face. 珍蒂畢絲凡爾賽舞會（1962）。感覺用了很多種香料混在一起。我就像擠在法革之前參加宮廷舞會的貴婦人們的腋下的門童，各種氣息撲面而來。
Guerlain Habit Rouge (1965). An aggressive and wild fragrance. Especially spicy and pungent. Essential for seeing ex-boyfriends and clients. 嬌蘭滿堂紅（1965）。具有攻擊性和野性的一支香水。特別辛辣刺鼻的味道。見前男友見甲方必噴。
Guerlain Samsara (1989). I do not remember. 嬌蘭聖莎拉（1989）。記不得了。
Guerlain L'Heure Bleue (1912). Powder mixed with alcohol. 嬌蘭藍調時光（1912）。脂粉混酒。
Guerlain Vol de Nuit (1933). Threw up in the flower bushes outside while drinking. 嬌蘭午夜飛行（1933）。喝酒吐外面花叢裡了。
Hermès Un Jardin sur le Nil (2005). I do not like aquatic notes. Very fresh, but feels like the lotus has rotted in the ground. 愛馬仕尼羅河花園（2005）。不喜歡水生調。很清新，但是感覺蓮花爛在地裡了。
Hermès Un Jardin sur la Lagune (2019). Something really has rotted in the ground. And it is together with dead fish and shrimp. 愛馬仕澙湖花園（2019）。真的有東西爛在地裡了，還跟魚蝦的屍體一起。
Hermès Un Jardin en Méditerranée (2003). Aquatic note, but the thing has not rotted yet. 愛馬仕地中海花園（2003）。水生調，但東西還沒爛。
Hermès Un Jardin sur le Toit (2011). The smell of walking into the flower section of a botanical garden. 愛馬仕屋頂花園（2011）。走進植物園花區的味道。
Hermès Un Jardin après la Mousson (2008). Forgot. 愛馬仕雨後花園（2008）。忘了。
Hermès Le Jardin de Monsieur Li (2015). My boss grows orchids in the office. 愛馬仕李先生花園（2015）。我老闆在辦公室養蘭花。
Tom Ford Fucking Fabulous (2017). I cannot stand incense anymore. TF法布勒斯（2017）。受不了焚香了。
Tom Ford Lost Cherry (2018). Feels too sour. The cherry note is still quite faithful; I just wish it had some sweetness. TF失落櫻桃（2018）。感覺太酸了。櫻桃味還是很還原的，就是我希望它帶點甜味。
Jean Paul Gaultier Scandal (2017). Berry honey purgatory. I am the self-loathing, self-abandoning, mass-produced teenage psychopath girl. 高緹耶絕密醜聞（2017）。莓果蜂蜜煉獄。我就是那個自厭自棄的十幾歲精神病量產女。
Zara 92 Champs-Élysées Flower Shop (2019?). Bought a 100ml bottle, very cheap. 1:1 replica of the smell of any flower shop I have entered since childhood. I really, really like it. Very pure floral. And it feels like a flower shop in a Nordic setting under towering snow mountains, near a stone-brick path, with a pine-framed glass door and a small blackboard hanging on it. Run by a gentle, elegant old lady. But it is discontinued. ZARA 巴黎香榭麗舍大街92號花店（2019？）。買了100ml正裝，很便宜。1：1復刻從小到大進過的任何一個花店的味道。很喜歡很喜歡。很純淨的花香，而且像是北歐高聳雪山下石磚路附近那種松木做框、玻璃門上掛小黑板的花店，有個溫和優雅的老奶奶經營。但是停產了。
Sabrina Carpenter Espresso (2024?). More like a fruity marshmallow than coffee. Sabrina Carpenter的espresso（2024？）。比起咖啡更像果味的棉花糖。
Penhaligon's Roaring Radcliff (2017). Also a men's fragrance. It has that pungent, primal quality I like. Very capable of arousing desire. 潘梅利根咆哮的拉狄克老夫（2017）。也是男香，有我喜欢的那種刺鼻原始感，很能勾起慾望。
Lattafa Choco Overdose (2021?). This is a very faithful chocolate cake scent that I really like. The only downside is a slight alcoholic note. Actually, overall I probably prefer sweet gourmands. But I also feel I need to maintain a professional, mature exterior image. Distressing. The fragrance belongs to Lattafa's "Give Me Gourmand" collection, with top notes of chocolate and sugar; heart notes of vanilla and coffee; and base notes of amber and musk. Lattafa的Choco Overdose（2021？）。這就是我很喜歡的非常還原的巧克力蛋糕味道。唯一美中不足的就是有一點酒精感。其實我總體可能更喜歡甜甜的美食調，但我又覺得我需要保持一個專業成熟點的外在形象。苦惱。這支香屬於Lattafa的「Give Me Gourmand」系列，前調是巧克力與糖；中調是香草與咖啡；後調是琥珀與麝香。
