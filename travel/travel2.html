Hong Kong·Phototaxis and Photophobia 香港· 趨光畏光

I have always wanted to find a chance to write about Hong Kong, yet always felt there was nothing to write. 一直想找個機會寫一寫香港，但又覺得沒有什麼可寫的。
I hate Hong Kong: skyscrapers packed together like frog eggs; traffic lights that beep day and night without distinction, and the moment I hear that sound my whole body trembles, my molars clench, but the tension in my masseter muscles only worsens my migraine. At Leighton Hill in Causeway Bay, the footpath is less than one person wide; you have to squeeze past pedestrians' sweat-dampened skin. I live near Times Square, where restaurants line up in a row, and after two steps you can see a cockroach corpse flipped on its back, crushed by the wheels of a delivery cart in the early morning. One midnight I opened the window, looked up and saw no moon, looked down and saw a huge rat climbing along the pipes. In my first semester all my classes were after four in the afternoon; by then the sun had passed through the buildings, and the shadow it cast was already enough to cover my short walk from home to the subway station. I shuttled through that shadow near the setting sun; sometimes my fate was no different from that of the cockroaches and rats. 我討厭香港：摩天大樓像青蛙卵一樣擠在一起；紅綠燈總在不分晝夜地滴滴叫，我一聽到那個聲音就渾身發抖，咬緊後槽牙，但咬肌緊張會加劇我的偏頭痛。銅鑼灣的禮頓山，步道不足一人寬，必須要貼著路人汗濕的肌膚經過。我家住時代廣場附近，一水兒餐館，走兩步就能看見四腳朝天，被清早送貨的推車輪子碾碎的蟑螂屍體。某個半夜我打開窗，抬頭看不見月亮，低頭一隻碩大的老鼠沿著管道攀爬。第一學期我的所有課都在下午四點之後，那時太陽穿過大樓，落下的陰影已經足夠覆蓋我從家到地鐵站的一小段路。我在那片靠近夕陽的陰影裡穿梭，有時我的際遇與蟑螂老鼠是一樣的。
I have never seen a concert in Hong Kong. I am like a creature that seeks light yet fears it. I need solitude, but after a long time, doing anything alone becomes tasteless. Last September I bought a single ticket to the Hong Kong Ocean Park. Five hundred dollars, not very expensive. I never made it; it has expired now. 我沒有在香港看過演唱會。我像一種趨光又畏光的生物。我需要獨處，但時間久了，一個人幹什麼又都索然無味。去年九月我買了一張香港海洋館的單人票。五百塊，不是很貴。我沒去成，現在已經過期了。
My experiences are truly乏善可陳. For the past twenty-some years I prided myself on being an interesting person; only now have I gradually discovered that I am a boring person. When in a foreign country, I easily develop good feelings toward others; studying abroad is actually a giant summer camp. In Hong Kong, I was attracted to a man. A tall, strong CBC with wheat-coloured skin and an infectious smile. I once found it hard to imagine him coming from Toronto—I heard the winters there are despairing. I always felt long winters should produce cold people, as if in this way I could explain part of my personality from my birthday. As with every crush, he and I never exchanged a single word. I only felt a strange impulse when I saw him confidently express his thoughts, or read the diary on his blog. My spine grew warm, my nose smelled soft bread fragrance, and then saliva began to secrete on both sides of my tongue. I have never actually been able to tell whether I wanted him as a lover, or wanted to replace his life. Desire and appetite, for me, are perhaps the same kind of desire. 我的經歷實在乏善可陳。過去二十多年我驕傲自己是一個有趣的人，現在才漸漸發現自己是一個無聊的人。在異國他鄉的時候，我很容易對別人產生好感，留學其實是一個巨大的夏令營。在香港，我被一位男士吸引。一個高大強壯的CBC，有小麥色的肌膚和有感染力的笑容。我一度很難想像他從多倫多來——聽說那裡的冬天讓人絕望。我總覺得漫長冬天應該長出冷漠的人，似乎這樣我就可以從我的生日解釋一部分的個性。和每一次的暗戀一樣，我和他沒有說過一句話。我只是在看見他自信地表達自己的想法，或是閱讀他博客上的日記時有一種奇怪的衝動。脊柱變得暖暖的，鼻子聞見柔軟的麵包香氣，接著我的舌頭兩側就開始分泌口水。我其實一直分不清到底是想他作為戀人，還是想取代他的人生。愛慾與食慾於我或許是同一種慾望。
From the moment I arrived in Hong Kong, I knew there was no future for me here, and I should never have placed my future in any one place. In my last week before leaving Hong Kong, one day I put on my earphones as usual and went to Fusion in Causeway Bay to buy chips. That day the shuffle played Eason Chan's "Best Friend." Suddenly my nose felt a little sour. As a writer, I perhaps naturally possess a terrifying voyeuristic desire toward anyone's life. So I also cannot tell whether what I felt then was the sorrow of parting, or the hunger in my stomach that had not been filled from afar. 從來到香港就知道這裡沒有我的未來，我也從不該在一個地方上寄託未來。我離開香港的最後一週，某天照常戴著耳機去銅鑼灣的fushion買薯片。那天隨機到陳奕迅的《最佳損友》，突然鼻子有點酸酸的。作為文手，我可能天然對任何人的人生都存在著可怕的窺探欲。於是我也不一樣分不清那時感受到的是離別的憂愁，還是胃裡遠遠沒被填滿的飢餓。
